Bangla News Article Classification

A Machine Learning project to classify Bengali news into categories like National, Science, Education, Sports, Politics, International, and more.

Introduction

The rapid growth of digital media has created a huge volume of Bengali news articles. Efficient classification helps improve information retrieval, content analysis, and recommendations.

Data Collection & Preprocessing

  • Shironaam Dataset (Hugging Face): Bengali news headlines
  • Bengali News Articles (Kaggle): Rich collection from newspapers
  • Potrika Bangla Newspaper (Kaggle): In-depth diverse reporting

Preprocessing steps included cleaning, merging, and balancing datasets.

Features

Traditional Features

  • IDF

Deep Learning Features

  • Tokenizer

Classifiers

The dataset was split (50% train, 25% validation, 25% test). Models used:

Logistic Regression

Predicts probability of news category. Works well with TF-IDF features for classification. 

from sklearn.linear_model import LogisticRegression
from sklearn.multiclass import OneVsRestClassifier

logistic_model = OneVsRestClassifier(
    LogisticRegression(max_iter=1000, solver="lbfgs")
)
