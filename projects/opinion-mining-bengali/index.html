Opinion Mining on Celebrity-Centric YouTube Comments in Bengali

A machine learning project that mines and interprets opinions from Bengali-language YouTube comments to bridge the language gap in sentiment analysis.

Introduction

In the age of digital media, public opinion is a powerful force, but what happens when entire languages are left out of the conversation? Our project, "Opinion Mining on Celebrity-Centric YouTube Comments in Bengali", directly addresses this critical gap. While sophisticated sentiment analysis tools exist for languages like English, they often fail to capture the rich and nuanced voices of regional languages, including Bengali.

We developed a sentiment analysis project focused on Bengali-language YouTube comments about celebrities. Our system classifies and interprets emotions in these comments to uncover public sentiment trends, contributing to a more inclusive digital landscape where all voices are represented.
